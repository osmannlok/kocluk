Bölüm C: Reading

Aşağıdaki metni okuyun ve soruları yanıtlayın:

Many people believe that sleep is simply an unproductive period during which our bodies rest. However, recent research suggests that sleep is far more dynamic and important. During sleep, the brain continues to process information, consolidate memories, and perform vital housekeeping tasks that support learning, immune function, and overall health.

Especially during the REM (rapid eye movement) stage, the brain is highly active. It is during REM sleep that dreams occur, and some scientists argue that this stage plays a crucial role in emotional regulation and mental health. On the other hand, non-REM sleep stages are believed to be responsible for physical restoration, such as muscle repair and growth, as well as waste removal from the brain.

Lack of sufficient sleep can lead to mood disturbances, poor cognitive performance, and weakened immunity. Over time, chronic sleep deprivation has been linked to serious health issues such as cardiovascular disease, diabetes, and obesity.
